# Three-minute demo script

Use a fresh, dedicated FalkorDB graph with model access configured. Show the real connection status. If using preview, identify it clearly and do not represent it as the final database-backed submission.

**0:00–0:20 — Problem.** “A policy changes once. Its outdated copies live on in support guides, onboarding workflows and agent memory. Ripple finds the consequences and prepares evidence-backed fixes.” Show the overview.

**0:20–0:50 — Change.** Open Change studio. Record an approved 7-day self-serve trial effective October 16, with the analysis date also October 16. Show 5 conflicts, 3 workflow reviews, 2 unaffected enterprise sources.

**0:50–1:20 — Why a graph.** Open the reminder workflow. Show `workflow → document → claim → old policy → topic`. Explain why it needs a human review rather than a speculative text patch. Open an enterprise source and show that its audience remains outside the change.

**1:20–1:55 — Act.** Open the getting started guide review. Show original vs proposed text and evidence. Approve the correction. Show revision 2 in the knowledge library, and before/after content in correction history.

**1:55–2:25 — Remember.** Reload the browser. Ask the self-serve trial duration: 7 days, sourced to the approved policy and decision. Ask for enterprise: 14 days. The persisted graph outlives the conversation.

**2:25–2:45 — More company context.** Ask about the refund window, then show its separate graph neighborhood. Ask about pricing to demonstrate a missing-knowledge response. Briefly show a proposed or future policy remaining inactive, if time permits.

**2:45–3:00 — Finish.** “Ripple turns a policy decision into traceable maintenance work. FalkorDB holds the dependencies, evidence, versions and review history.” Show repository and setup link.

Optional longer demo: paste a new fictional old-policy document, extract and review its claim, save it, and refresh an existing decision analysis to show newly discovered impact.
