## Explain the agent and graph

Gemini extracts candidate claims and selects a restricted read-only policy tool. The graph's approved version, audience and date determine authoritative values. `ASSERTS` connects a source to its claim; `DEPENDS_ON` connects knowledge to policy context and other knowledge. Native FalkorDB traversal discovers indirect effects; dependency alone does not prove a contradiction.

Human approval gates source repair and publication. The original source, previous claims, decisions and correction evidence remain. A workspace revision precondition prevents a stale server request restoring old content. A refresh on 409 is deliberate conflict handling.

## Be accurate about boundaries

The public demo supports four duration-policy areas in disposable visitor sandboxes. It includes live Gemini and native FalkorDB, evidence, history, import, review, export and controlled publishing. Public company accounts with recovery/invites, arbitrary contract interpretation, autonomous external-site editing and automatic scheduled website monitoring are outside this submission's implemented scope. Persistent context means saved policies and reviewed replies/history, not unrestricted conversational memory. Sandbox access expires after two hours; physical cleanup occurs later.

No code or test can promise zero future defects. The release checks provide specific evidence for this workflow. Provider quota/network availability and the free Cloud instance remain external limits.
